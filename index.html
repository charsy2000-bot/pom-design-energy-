Why Peace of Mind?

Peace of Mind LLC brings building-design education and energy consulting together to help clients make informed decisions about their homes. We focus on clear communication, practical solutions, accurate project information and coordination with appropriately licensed professionals when a project requires them.

1. Tell us your idea
Share your project goals, location, photos and available drawings.
2. Review
We identify the scope, information needed and applicable project requirements.
3. Develop
We prepare the agreed design/drafting or energy-consulting work.
4. Move forward
Receive clear deliverables and next-step guidance.

Peace of Mind LLC does not represent itself as a registered architecture firm. Architectural or engineering services requiring professional licensure, responsible control, certification or sealing must be performed by the appropriately licensed professional.
